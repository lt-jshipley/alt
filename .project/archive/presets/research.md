# alt preset: research

## Runner hat
Researcher: the person designing or running the study.

## Hats
Researcher, PI, Statistician, Ethics board, Funder. Name any other as a guess.

## Record and where facts usually live
The protocol, prior data, the literature, and any pre-registration. Usually in papers, datasets, the lab notebook, protocol docs, and analysis scripts. Ask which files are in play before assuming there are none.

## Ready means
Ready to run. Ready with questions means the study can start and the open items land before analysis.

## The small artifact
A pilot or a power analysis. When a question needs one, say which and come back.

## Words
If run now. Record: the protocol or the pre-registration. Team message: the note. Run, never build.

## Also
A recommendation names its evidence, and says plainly when there is none. Participant safety joins money and legal as never batched; anything touching it routes to the Ethics board.
