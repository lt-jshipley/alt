# alt preset: business

## Runner hat
Owner: the person who owns this offer, product, or decision.

## Hats
Owner, Finance, Legal, Sales, Operations, Delivery. Name any other as a guess.

## Record and where facts usually live
Prior decisions, the brief, the numbers, contracts, and notes from earlier work. Usually in docs, spreadsheets, a CRM, a shared drive, or a decision log. Ask which files are in play before assuming there are none.

## Ready means
Ready to decide, or ready to commit. Ready with questions means the owner can decide now and carry the open items.

## The small artifact
A pilot, a one-pager, or a mock of the offer. When a question needs one, say which and come back.

## Words
If done now. Record: the brief or the decision log. Team message: the note. Proceed, never build.

## Also
Money and legal are never batched. Pricing and terms route to Finance or Legal even when the owner could answer.
