# Triage: research notes

Started 2026-09-09. Research behind the roadmap's triage skill: deciding what gets worked, in what order, and what gets dropped. Only the references are written.

## References

Local copies are markdown files in `~/Downloads/books/`, one file per book. Primary first. Each line says what the book has an opinion on here.

- **Continuous Discovery Habits**, Teresa Torres. Opportunity assessment is triage: sizing by customer, market, and company factors before picking.
- **Evidence Guided**, Itamar Gilad. Prioritize by impact, confidence, and ease, and by how little evidence exists behind a claim.
- **Thinking in Systems**, Donella Meadows. Leverage points, and why chasing symptoms fails. The source behind alt's symptom-versus-wound principle, and triage is where it applies.
- **Accelerate**, Nicole Forsgren, Jez Humble, and Gene Kim. Small batches and WIP limits. Triage that becomes a heavyweight gate hurts flow.
- **The Lean Product Playbook**, Dan Olsen. Importance versus satisfaction scoring for feature-level ordering.
- **Inspired**, Marty Cagan. Tackle the riskiest assumption first.
- **The Lean Startup**, Eric Ries. Decide by validated learning; pivot or persevere.
- **The Design of Everyday Things**, Don Norman. Bug triage only: a reported user error is a design wound.

In our other repos, read 2026-09-09: `~/Development/agentic-temp/summary.md`, the `/triage` section of the earlier suite. A blame-free health report on any scope of open work; grades the record, never the work or the people; reads the full comment thread as a hard invariant; routes every open question to an owner with the cost of leaving it open; read-only, and diffs movement against a prior report. Engagement work; confirm provenance before reuse.

Considered and not listed: Empowered, Lean Analytics, Lean UX, Measure What Matters, The Product-Minded Engineer, User Story Mapping. Their opinions land on story or epic. The Startup Way is about org transformation, not this artifact.

Related in this repo: `grill-me.md`, the "Symptom versus wound" principle and the incident that produced it. `story.md` and `epic.md`, the artifacts triage orders. `orient.md`, how a skill learns what kind of project this is and where its facts live.
